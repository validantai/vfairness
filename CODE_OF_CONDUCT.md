# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the vfairness community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to conduct@validant.ai. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
