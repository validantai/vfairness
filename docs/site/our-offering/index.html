The Architecture for Trust & Agency

Three organisations form an integrated architecture spanning foundation, system, and execution — together defining, encoding, and embedding trust in AI-driven organisations.

iceberg.digital
Defining the Structural Logic

Explores sovereignty, governance, identity, reciprocity, and institutional design. Reframes trust as a systems property shaped by deep design decisions. Trust must be defined structurally before it can be implemented operationally.

validant.ai
Encoding the Systemic Capability

Transforms fairness and governance principles into structured capability frameworks. Embeds fairness objectives, intervention strategies, regulatory alignment, and monitoring logic into the AI lifecycle. Trust becomes measurable and scalable.

Glinz & Company GmbH
Embedding in Enterprise Reality

Partners with executive teams to redesign AI-enabled operating models. Aligns governance, analytics, product development, and leadership accountability. Trust becomes anchored in institutional practice.

Validant AI Fairness Platform

A separate, hosted product for teams that want managed assurance beyond what a library provides. It is another way to work with us, not part of the library itself. Patrons who sponsor at USD 50/mo or more get Pro included; anyone else can subscribe directly.

Causal DAG Builder

Visual directed acyclic graph editor. Drag-and-drop variables, draw causal edges, auto-generate from proxy analysis, and compute path-specific fairness effects.

Decision Flip Testing

Automated perturbation of protected attributes with flip rate computation, severity scoring, SHAP/LIME attribution, and API endpoint probing for black-box models.

Counterfactual Suite

Twin test framework, counterfactual data generation from causal DAGs, group-level counterfactual analysis, and exportable explanation cards.

10-Phase Assessment Wizard

Step-by-step guided workflow automating the full EU AI Act compliance audit — from data validation through to model card generation.

What-If Simulator

Multi-dimensional real-time exploration of thresholds, constraints, and calibration methods. Pareto frontier search and shareable stakeholder views.

Evidence Packages

One-click export of the entire assessment — SVGs, metrics, DAGs, audit trail, model card — zipped and cryptographically signed for regulatory submission.
